Privacy Policy

Last Train from Hollow Pass · Last updated 28 October 2026

Last Train from Hollow Pass does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or session recording.
  • The game does not connect to the internet once it has loaded. The Android app requests no permissions.
  • No camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos are accessed.

What is saved on your device

Your current chapter, clues found, evidence-board connections, best times, star ratings and sound settings. This information stays on your device and is never sent to the developer.

Deleting your data

In a browser, clear this website's data in your browser settings. On Android, uninstall the game or clear its storage.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It collects no personal data from anyone.
